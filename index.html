Questions

Worth knowing

Will you do a single item?

Yes. One bracket, one broken stainless fitting, one gate panel — all fine. There's no minimum order.

I don't have a drawing. Can you still help?

Send a photo with a tape measure or ruler in the shot, or a sketch with the dimensions written on it. We'll draw it up and send it back for you to confirm before anything gets cut.

What's the difference between the 7kW and 2kW machines?

The 7kW is the cutting machine — more power means thicker plate and faster, cleaner cuts. The 2kW handheld unit does the welding and the cleaning, where you want a controlled, narrow heat input rather than raw power.

Why laser welding instead of TIG or MIG?

The heat goes into a much smaller area, so thin sheet doesn't warp and stainless doesn't discolour across the whole panel. The bead is finer and needs far less grinding and polishing, which usually brings the total cost down on visible work.

Does laser cleaning damage the metal underneath?

No. It removes the rust, scale or coating on the surface and stops at the parent metal. That makes it safe on threads, machined faces, moulds and thin panels where sandblasting would eat into the material.

Do you deliver outside Gauteng?

Yes, we courier countrywide. The courier cost is quoted with the job so you can see it upfront.

How do I pay?

EFT on approval of the quote. Larger jobs can be split into a deposit and a balance on collection.
